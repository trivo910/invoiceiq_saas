### Creating an Invoice in Minutes

Navigate to **Invoices → New Invoice**.

**Step 1 — Your Customer, Instantly**

Start typing a customer's name. InvoiceIQ searches your database and suggests matches. Select one — and their full address, GSTIN, and billing details fill in automatically. No typing addresses from scratch. No copy-paste errors. The right details, every time.

First time billing this customer? Type their details manually, or add them to your Customers list first — and they will be waiting for you on every future invoice.

**Step 2 — Invoice Identity**

- **Invoice Number** — Auto-generated in your chosen format (e.g., `INV-2025-0001`). Sequential, unique, never repeated.
- **Invoice Date** — Pre-filled with today.
- **Due Date** — Set when you expect payment. Once this date passes, the invoice automatically turns **Overdue** — you never have to manually chase the status.
- **Place of Supply** — The state of delivery. InvoiceIQ uses this to automatically determine whether to apply CGST+SGST or IGST. You don't need to think about it.

**Step 3 — Add What You Sold**

Click **Add Item** for each product or service on this invoice.

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|--------------|
| Item Name | What you sold or delivered |
| HSN/SAC Code | Government classification code (important for GST filing) |
| Qty | How many units |
| Rate | Price per unit |
| Discount % | Per-item discount, if any |
| GST % | 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% |

As you type, InvoiceIQ calculates every number live — taxable amount, CGST, SGST, IGST. No formulas. No chance of a wrong calculation reaching your customer.

Add as many items as needed. Remove any item with one click.

**Step 4 — Overall Discount (Optional)**

Want to give your customer a special deal on the total invoice? Enter an invoice-level discount as a percentage or a flat rupee amount. GST adjusts automatically.

**Step 5 — Your Message to the Customer**

Add **Notes** (payment instructions, a thank-you line) and **Terms & Conditions** (your payment policy, late fee terms). These appear at the bottom of the printed invoice — small details that make a big impression.

**Step 6 — Send It**

- **Save as Draft** — Not ready yet? Save and come back. Drafts are private until you send them.
- **Save & Send Email** — Invoice is saved, converted to PDF, and the email window opens — ready for your customer's address and a personal note.

GST compliance is non-negotiable. But it doesn't have to be a crisis at the end of every quarter. InvoiceIQ generates every report your CA needs — in the exact format they need it — in seconds.

### Choose Your Period

Every report lets you filter by:
- This month / Last month
- This quarter / Last quarter
- This year / Last year
- **Custom date range** — your choice, any period

---

### GSTR-1 Summary — Your Complete Sales Picture

A full list of all sales invoices for the selected period. Each invoice shows taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, total tax, and grand total. Automatically split into:

- **B2B** — Sales to registered businesses (customers with GSTIN)
- **B2C** — Sales to individuals and unregistered buyers

Hand this to your CA and the bulk of GSTR-1 is done.

---

### B2B Report — Know Every Registered Client You Billed

One row per registered customer. GSTIN, state, invoice count, taxable amount, full GST breakdown, and total. Sorted by value — so your largest accounts are always at the top.

This is the data that goes directly into the B2B section of your GSTR-1 return.

---

### B2C Report — Your Retail and Consumer Sales

Invoices to unregistered buyers, grouped by state and whether the sale was intra-state or inter-state. Exactly what your CA needs for the B2C section of GSTR-1.

---

### HSN/SAC Summary — Product-Level Compliance Made Easy

All your sales grouped by HSN or SAC code. Total quantity, taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST — per code, per GST rate. This is **Schedule B** in GSTR-1, which many businesses struggle to prepare. InvoiceIQ generates it automatically.

The only requirement: make sure you filled in HSN/SAC codes on your items.

---

### Tax Liability Report — Plan Before You Pay

See exactly how much output tax you owe — broken down by GST rate (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) and by **month**. No surprises on payment day. No scrambling to calculate what you owe. You see it clearly, well in advance.

## Common Questions, Straight Answers

**Q: I sell to customers in other states. How does InvoiceIQ handle that?**
Automatically. When your state code and the customer's state code are different, InvoiceIQ charges IGST instead of CGST+SGST. You don't configure anything — it detects it from the Place of Supply on each invoice.

**Q: A customer paid part now and will pay the rest next month. What do I do?**
Record the first payment as you normally would. InvoiceIQ marks the invoice **Partial** and shows the balance due. When the second payment comes in, record that too. The invoice becomes **Paid** automatically. Full history, full audit trail.

**Q: I sent an invoice with a wrong amount. Can I fix it?**
Yes. Open the invoice, click Edit, fix it, and save. If you already emailed the original to the customer, send a revised copy with a note explaining the correction.

**Q: I want to cancel an invoice that was raised by mistake.**
Change the status to **Cancelled**. The invoice stays in the system for your records but disappears from all reports and totals. Nothing is ever deleted — only voided.

**Q: My accountant asks for a GST report every month. How fast can I send it?**
Under a minute. Open GST Reports, select the period, click Export Excel. You have a multi-sheet workbook with GSTR-1 summary, B2B, B2C, HSN summary, and tax liability — all in one file. Email it and move on.

**Q: What is Place of Supply and why does it matter?**
It is the state where goods are delivered or services are consumed — required by GST law on every invoice. InvoiceIQ uses it to automatically determine whether CGST+SGST (same state as your business) or IGST (different state) applies. You select it; InvoiceIQ does the rest.
