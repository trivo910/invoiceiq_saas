# Legal page sample notes

**Status:** fictional working sample, prepared 29 October 2026. It is not legal advice, an approved policy, or the live operator's legal notice. The public pages intentionally keep a prominent `SAMPLE DRAFT` warning and use the reserved `example.invalid` contact domain. Do not set `LAUNCH_LEGAL_APPROVED=true` for this copy.

## Sample facts and limits

- `InvoiceIQ Sample Operator` is invented placeholder text, not a claimed registered entity.
- `legal@example.invalid` is a deliberately non-deliverable example address, not a complaint or data-rights channel.
- India is an illustrative jurisdiction because the product supports Indian GST invoicing. The state, competent courts, legal entity, tax position, contract form, and dispute process are undecided.
- The terms use explicitly labeled example assumptions for account responsibility, acceptable use, content rights and possible subscription cancellation. They do not assert approved prices, refund rights, service levels, warranties, liability cap, or a specific retention period.
- The privacy sample reflects inspected features: account and membership data, company settings, customer/invoice/payment data, provider identifiers/metadata, audit events, exports and session/security information. It identifies deployment, mail, residency, retention, grievance, legal basis and data-subject request handling as items that still need verification.
- Current code soft-deletes workspace roots and retains their data for platform recovery; no permanent purge is implemented. The sample calls this out rather than promising erasure.
- Razorpay billing is configurable but account-backed operation is unresolved. Hosting and SMTP production providers are not selected. No third-party processor list should be treated as complete.

## Regulatory reference trail

The draft links to official Gazette/India Code/MeitY source documents from the public legal pages. Research checked on 29 October 2026 found that the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 have phased commencement notifications. The Act notification (G.S.R. 843(E), 13 Nov 2025) schedules different provisions immediately, after one year, and after eighteen months. The Rules notification (G.S.R. 846(E), 13 Nov 2025) also phases provisions. This note does not determine whether any provision applies to the eventual service or operator on a particular date; qualified counsel must check the latest Gazette, amendments, sector rules and actual processing model.
